RenCOSMOS is a digital studio focused on building apps and educational tools. RenCosmos is the composition of Ren, from ancient Egyptian, and Cosmos, the entirety of the universe. In ancient Egypt, the word "Ren" referred to someone's name. Names were the eternal breath of being, an immortal spark inscribed in the ​firmament. They believed that as long as the ren was spoken and remembered, the individual's soul would flourish in eternity.
