Anatomy Atlas & Diagnosis Game

An educational medical application designed to support students of medicine, nursing, and all health-related fields.

Anatomy Atlas: Explore the human body through an atlas organized by anatomical systems, featuring professional medical illustrations and detailed anatomical structures.

Pathology Section: Learn about diseases and medical conditions with educational resources and a symptom-based search system designed to support medical study.

Guess the Diagnosis: Test your clinical knowledge with medical cases, medical images, multiple-choice questions, lives, coins, and global and country-based leaderboards.

The application is regularly updated with improvements and educational content.

Important Notice: This application is intended solely for educational and informational purposes. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical care or clinical judgment.
